# Demographic Differences Among ​SNAP Participants in Eat Well Be Well Program Awareness
This study aimed to evaluate the association between awareness of the Eat Well Be Well (EWBW) Program and demographic factors such as household size, race and ethnicity, and socioeconomic status (SES). The goal was to identify which population subgroups should be further targeted in EWBW program marketing. 

Data was obtained from the RIPHI EWBW survey data and cleaned up to remove missing values and only include eligible participants in the analysis.

We used descrptive statistics, bivariate and multivariate graphs, and logistic regression to examine the relationship of awareness and number of children between various covariates.

Number of Children, Ethnicity, and length on SNAP were significant predictors of awareness of the program.
